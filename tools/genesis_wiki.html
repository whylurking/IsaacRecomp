GenesisConsole

A console inside the game, like the one in Rebirth: type a command during a run to give yourself items, spawn anything, change your stats, become invincible, open every door or jump to any room or floor. This page explains everything it can do.

While GenesisConsole is on, nothing is saved. No achievements, no unlocks, no statistics, no collection, and changes to the options are not kept either. The game can't unlock anything during those sessions. Your save file isn't touched: turn the mod off and everything is as it was. A small GENESIS - NOT SAVED in the corner reminds you during runs.

Turning it on

The mod is the file GenesisConsole.mod in the game's mods folder. Open tools/mod_manager.html, choose the game's folder, switch GenesisConsole on and save. Or write it in isaac.ini: mods = genesis (with StatOn too: mods = staton, genesis). Then start the game: log.txt says GenesisConsole is on.

It works in the three games (the original, Wrath of the Lamb and Eternal Edition) and on every platform: Windows, Linux, Android and Switch.

Opening and closing it

It opens during a run (not in the menus or during a cutscene). The game pauses while it's open.

Keyboard

The key above Tab, left of 1 (`, º, ² or ^, depending on your keyboard). Close with the same key or Esc. Another key can be chosen in the options.

Controller

Back / View (Xbox), Share / Create (PlayStation), − (Switch). With the mod on, that button opens the console instead of pausing (Start / + still pause). Close with B or the same button.

Touch screen

Touch the screen with three fingers at once (Android, Switch in handheld mode). Close the same way, or with Android's back button.

Typing commands

Type a command and press Enter. The line under the box shows what you can type next; Tab completes it (press it again for the next match). What it says is shown in green, mistakes in red.

Keyboard

  • Tab complete a command or a name
  • ↑ ↓ earlier commands
  • ← → Home End move in the line
  • PgUp PgDn or the mouse wheel: scroll the text
  • Ctrl+V paste, Ctrl+U clear the line

Controller

  • A: the on-screen keyboard (Switch, Android); on Switch what you type runs when you press OK
  • X: run the line, Y: complete it
  • D-pad ↑ ↓: earlier commands (run one again with X), ← →: move
  • LB / RB (L / R): scroll

Touch

  • Tap the screen: the phone's keyboard appears; its Enter runs the line
  • Three fingers again: close

Your last 100 commands are kept in saves/genesis_history.txt: next time, ↑ (or the D-pad) brings them back, which makes it easy to repeat a set-up with a controller.

Names

Items, trinkets, cards, enemies and bosses can be written by their number or by name, in any case and without punctuation: give mom's knife, give moms knife and give 114 are the same. A part of the name is enough when only one thing matches (give knife); if several do, the console lists them with their numbers. find <text> searches everything.

What can be spawned

  • An enemy or a boss, by name or number: spawn pooter, spawn 14, spawn gurdy jr. Enemies appear a little away from you; bosses can be spawned in any room. A number with a decimal asks the game for a variant (spawn 10.1: the game decides what the variant is for each monster; for most, a champion-like version).
  • A pickup by its name: spawn key, spawn soulheart 3 (list).
  • An item on a pedestal: spawn item <item>. To have it straight away: give.
  • A trinket: spawn trinket <name> (Wrath of the Lamb, Eternal Edition).
  • A card: spawn card <name>, e.g. spawn card fool, spawn card joker.
  • A pill: spawn pill (any colour) or spawn pill 3 (colour 1-6). What each colour does changes every run, as in the game.

Adding a number at the end spawns several: spawn gaper 5, spawn item 7 2.

      Stats

      speed, tears, damage, range and shotspeed fix the stat: items that would change it no longer do, until you write reset (damage reset). They last for the rest of the session, through new runs; stats marks them with *, and the console's title bar lists them. luck just sets your luck, which items keep changing as usual.

      • tears is in shots per second, like StatOn shows it (without the console, the game usually stops at 5).
      • damage is the damage stat; items that multiply each tear's damage still do.
      • range and shotspeed apply to the tears you fire from then on.

      With the StatOn mod on too, you see your stats on screen while you change them.

      Options

      In mods/GenesisConsole.mod, or in the mod manager:

      OptionDefault
      keygraveThe keyboard key that opens it: grave (the key above Tab) or a key name: F1, F2, F4...F10, Insert, Home... (F3, F11 and F12 are taken by the game).
      pause10: the game goes on while the console is open.
      opacity85How dark the console's background is, 30-100 (%).
      badge10 hides "GENESIS - NOT SAVED" during runs.

      Good to know

      • Everything the console does uses the game's own code: items given with give work exactly as if picked up, teleports play the game's teleport, floors are made by the game.
      • Some things can't be undone by the console (an item's one-time effect, a floor already left). It's a sandbox: nothing of it is saved anyway.
      • God mode, doors and fixed stats stay on through new runs until you turn them off or close the game.
      • Commands that act on the run don't work in the menus, during a cutscene or a boss's introduction: the console says so.
      • Too many monsters at once (spawn 20 of a boss...) can slow the game down, as it would in Flash.
      • To play normally again, turn the mod off in the mod manager (or remove genesis from mods = in isaac.ini) and restart the game.
